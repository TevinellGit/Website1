Some TF2 classes

Scout
The Scout is a fast, fragile, and aggressive offensive class who relies on speed, hit-and-run tactics, and the ability to capture objectives faster than any other mercenary to flank enemies and disrupt the opposing team.
Heavy
The Heavy is a massive, slow-moving Russian mercenary who utilizes high-damage miniguns and the largest health pool in the game to serve as a formidable defensive anchor and frontline damage-dealer.
Soldier
The Soldier is a crazed, ultra-patriotic American mercenary from the Midwest who serves as a versatile, all-purpose assault class, famous for using his Rocket Launcher to deal explosive splash damage and launch himself through the air.
